Privacy Policy

Plankfall: Screw Puzzle
Effective Date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Plankfall: Screw Puzzle ("we", "us", "the App", package com.kings.plankfall) handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: Plankfall is a screw puzzle game. Your progress stays on your device. We do not require an account, do not run servers that receive personal data, do not use analytics, do not sell your data, and have no real-money purchases. The App shows ads from Google AdMob, and uses the internet only to load those ads. Google and its ad partners collect some device data to show and measure ads (see section 4).

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Player Name & Avatar: an optional display name and an avatar you pick from preset options, shown in the App's leagues, teams, and profile screens.
  • Levels You Create: boards and level packs you build in the level editor.

These features run entirely on your device: the leagues, teams, and other players you see are simulated by the App, and nothing you enter is uploaded or shown to real people. The App never asks for your real name, email address, phone number, photos, or any other personal information, and there are no online accounts or chat.

B. Information Stored on Your Device

To save your progress, the App keeps a small amount of data in its private storage on your device:

  • Game Progress: levels completed, stars, events, and season progress.
  • In-Game Items: virtual coins, boosters, lives, and rewards. These have no cash value.
  • Player Profile & Creations: your chosen display name, avatar, and the levels you created.
  • App Settings: sound, music, haptics, language, and similar preferences.

This data is never sent to us. We cannot see it. Google's consent SDK also stores your ad consent choice on your device.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Real name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Crash reports, usage analytics, or browsing history (we use no analytics)

3. Data Storage & Security

All App data is stored in Android's private app storage. It is accessible only to the App and is protected by the Android operating system's sandboxing. We do not operate any server that receives data from the App.

If you have turned on Android device backup, Android may include the App's saved progress in your personal Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we have no access to it.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses one third-party service: Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform consent SDK). It shows optional "watch an ad" rewards (for example a free booster, a revive, a free chest or double coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Full-screen ads never appear in the first 10 levels or after a loss.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, and ad-interaction data (for example which ads were shown or tapped). Where you have consented, they may also use it to personalize ads.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • US state privacy laws: where applicable, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • How Google uses data from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The App uses no analytics, crash reporting, payment processing, or other tracking tools.

5. Permissions

The App requests internet access, used only to load ads, and the advertising ID permission added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The game itself works without a connection. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information. The only data that leaves your device is the advertising data described in section 4, which Google AdMob collects directly to serve ads.

7. Data Retention

Your game data stays on your device until you reset it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own retention policies.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Using Settings → Reset progress in the App,
  • Clearing the App's data in your device Settings, or
  • Uninstalling the App.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if a future version of the App adds new features or services. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Where the law requires it, we will also ask for your consent in the App. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
