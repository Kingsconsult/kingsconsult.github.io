Privacy Policy

Whirlo: Marble Sort Puzzle
Effective Date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Whirlo: Marble Sort Puzzle ("we", "us", "the App", package com.kings.whirlo) collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: Whirlo is a marble sort puzzle game. Your progress is stored on your device. We do not require an account, do not run servers that receive personal data, do not sell your data, and have no real-money purchases. The App shows ads from Google AdMob, and uses the internet only to load those ads. Google and its ad partners collect some device data to show and measure ads (see section 4).

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

None. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number, photos, or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no chat, and no leaderboards.

B. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Game Progress: levels unlocked, stars, the level in progress, win streak, Zen boards cleared, event tokens, achievements, and play statistics (for example levels won and time played).
  • In-Game Items: virtual coins, boosters, owned skins, and chest and reward progress. These have no cash value.
  • App Settings: sound, music, haptics, language, colourblind symbols, reduced motion, reduce flashes, left-handed layout, graphics quality, and similar preferences.
  • Test Group Number: a random number used to place players into groups when we test game settings. It is not linked to you and never leaves your device.

None of this data is sent to us. We cannot see it. Google's consent SDK also stores your ad consent choice on your device.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Real name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Payment card details
  • Usage analytics or browsing history (we use no analytics)

3. Data Storage & Security

All App data is stored in Android's private app storage. It is accessible only to the App and is protected by the Android operating system's sandboxing. We do not operate any server that receives personal data from the App.

If you have turned on Android device backup, Android may include the App's saved progress in your personal Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we have no access to it.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses one third-party service: Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform consent SDK). It shows optional "watch an ad" rewards and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Full-screen ads never appear in the first levels, in Zen mode or in daily challenges.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, and ad-interaction data (for example which ads were shown or tapped). Where you have consented, they may also use it to personalize ads.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • US state privacy laws: where applicable, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • How Google uses data from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The App uses no analytics, crash reporting, payment processing, or other tracking tools.

5. Permissions

The App requests internet access, used only to load ads, and the advertising ID permission added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The game itself works without a connection. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information. The only data that leaves your device is the advertising data described in section 4, which Google AdMob collects directly to serve ads.

7. Data Retention

  • Local data: kept on your device until you reset it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention policies.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Using Settings → Reset progress in the App (clears game progress),
  • Clearing the App's data in your device Settings, or
  • Uninstalling the App.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information (for example by email), please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if a future version of the App adds new features or services. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Where the law requires it, we will also ask for your consent in the App. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
